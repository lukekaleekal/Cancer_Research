Model assumptions and reading the animation

50 Izhikevich neurons: 40 excitatory (a=0.02, b=0.20, c=−65, d=8) and 10 inhibitory (a=0.03, b=0.25, c=−60, d=4). Directed connections are sampled independently with probability 0.18; no self-connections. Seed: 1937. Integration step: 0.25 ms; voltage samples: 1 ms.

Background input: 5–7 model units per neuron. Excitatory synaptic increments: +1.2 units, decay time 5 ms; inhibitory increments: −2.5 units, decay time 10 ms. Assumed conversion: 1 model unit = 0.1 nA, equivalent to choosing a 100 pF capacitance for current scaling. This is an explicit modeling assumption, not a measured calibration.

The pulse applies −18 model units (−1.8 nA) to inhibitory neurons from 400 to 600 ms. Control and pulse runs share initial states, background drives, and connectivity. Graph 5 shows only synaptic currents. Firing rates use trailing 50 ms windows. Correlations use 20 ms spike-count bins across the full 1 s run; heatmaps and summary statistics show the full run independently of playback.

Bright neurons fired recently. Traveling dots illustrate communication, not physical conduction speed. Post-pulse spikes alone do not establish intrinsic rebound spiking. At 40 ms per real second, the 1 s simulation plays in approximately 25 seconds.

Reference: Izhikevich (2003), Simple Model of Spiking Neurons. D3 7.9.0 loads from jsDelivr; an internet connection is required.
